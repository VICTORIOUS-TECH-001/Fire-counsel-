# Fire Counsel brand film source pack

The finished downloadable film is `../Fire-Counsel-brand-film.mp4` (1280 × 720, H.264/AAC, 45 seconds).

## Included production assets

- `law-library.jpg`, `exam-hall.jpg`, `call-to-bar.jpg` — photorealistic AI-generated illustrative scenes made for this film; they are not documentary footage of identified people or a specific ceremony.
- `fire-counsel-narration.mp3` — original English voice-over narration.
- `../tools/make_brand_film.py` — reproducible frame animation, brand treatment, and original synthesized ambient bed. The Fire Counsel AI screen is a designed product vignette based on the real feature labels and copy in `ai.html`; it is illustrative rather than a captured signed-in session.

To regenerate from the repository root, install Pillow and imageio-ffmpeg and run:

```sh
python3 tools/make_brand_film.py
```
