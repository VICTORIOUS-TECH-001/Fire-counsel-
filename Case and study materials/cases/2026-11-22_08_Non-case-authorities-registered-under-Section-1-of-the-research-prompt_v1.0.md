## G. Ratio decidendi

Section 5 of the Law Reform (Contracts) Law Cap 66, Laws of Lagos State: 'No contract to which this section applies shall be enforceable by action unless the contract or some memorandum or note in respect thereof is in writing and is signed by the party to be charged therewith or by some other person lawfully authorised by him.' VERIFIED as reproduced in Tejumola. It is a rule of evidence, not of formation: as Kay J put it in Bristol, Cardiff and Swansea Aerated Bread Co v Maggs (1890) 44 Ch D 616 at 622, 'the question whether the two letters relied on were a complete contract, or were only steps in a negotiation, is altogether independent of the Statute of Frauds.'

## H. The exact legal rule

See above. Note that the provision reproduced by the Supreme Court is the Lagos State law; a different State may have its own Contracts Law, and the applicable version and date must be checked before it is asserted in a problem answer.

## I. Exceptions, limits and qualifications

Section 5 does not apply to every contract; it applies to the classes of contract within its terms (notably contracts for the sale or other disposition of land), subject to the recognised exceptions such as part performance and the doctrine of equitable estoppel. Do not assume the Lagos provision applies throughout Nigeria — it is a State law.

## J. Application to the subject

Governs the formal validity of land contracts that would otherwise be concluded by offer and acceptance, and is the reason Tejumola's outcome, though described by Obaseki JSC as an instance of hard cases making bad law, was legally correct.

## L. Examination significance

Cite the Lagos provision only for Lagos transactions and check for the equivalent law in the relevant State. Do not present it as a federal statute. The commonest mistake is to attribute a general writing requirement to Nigerian contract law as a whole.

**Rule / ratio.** A contract within the section is unenforceable by action unless it, or a signed memorandum or note of it, is in writing.
